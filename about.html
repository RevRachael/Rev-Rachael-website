About

Hello, I’m Rachael

I'm Rachael Dilling, a minister in the Centers for Spiritual Living tradition and Assistant Minister at CSL St. George. Science of Mind is my spiritual home, and I hold a master's degree in Consciousness Studies from Holmes Institute. The question running underneath all of my work is a simple one: what do we do with what we notice about ourselves?

Since 2017 I've served as a hospice chaplain and bereavement coordinator in Las Vegas. That work taught me to listen longer than feels comfortable, to trust silence, and to understand that presence is often the whole ministry. Sometimes the most spiritual thing I can do is pull up a chair.

I publish an essay on Medium nearly every week. Most of them begin somewhere ordinary, with a conversation that stayed with me, a reaction I didn't expect from myself, or a chicken with a stubborn streak. I follow those moments into the larger questions of how we live and love, and I've come to see inner work and world healing as the same work at different scales.

I also founded Loving Spaces, an independent living home for senior women in Las Vegas, where community is a daily practice rather than a topic. Community is the lens I bring to everything, from the Sunday platform to a quiet bedside.
