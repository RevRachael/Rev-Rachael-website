Read my essays Watch my talks

Spiritual practice lives in the ordinary moments.

The conversation I replay, the boundary I have to keep holding, the fear that shows up right before something that matters. Those are the moments most of us would rather move past, and they are exactly where I write and speak from.

My work is noticing what those moments reveal about us, and what we choose to do with what we notice. I bring that question to the Sunday platform at Centers for Spiritual Living St. George, to the bedside as a hospice chaplain, and to the page every week.
