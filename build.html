Technology in service of community

Community is the lens I bring to every ministry decision, and technology is no exception. A good website, a simple store, or a quiet bit of automation should give a minister more time with people, not more time at a keyboard.

I studied graphic design at UNLV, and I've built about half a dozen ministry websites over the years. I'm also an assistant minister, so I understand the rhythm of a center from the inside: the Sunday announcements, the prayer requests, the classes, the giving, and the volunteers who keep it all running.

I work with the platforms many centers already use. The CSL St. George website is built in Tithe.ly, which brings the website, online giving, and the church app together in one place.
